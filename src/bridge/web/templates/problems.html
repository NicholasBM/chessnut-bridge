How to fix it

Put the SD card in a computer. On the small partition that appears (bootfs), create or edit chessnut-bridge.conf:

chesscom_username=your-chess-com-username
web_password=a-password-for-this-page

Then put the card back and power the appliance on. The password protects this page, which can submit moves in your games — so it will not be served at all until one is set.
