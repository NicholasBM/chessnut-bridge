What happens to it

This is your real Chess.com password — the appliance uses it to sign in the same way a browser does, because reading your games needs no login but submitting a move does.

Ticking the box stores it encrypted on the card, in /var/lib/chessnut-bridge, with the key kept in a different directory. That is what lets the bridge carry on working when the session expires weeks from now with nobody here to retype it. It is not protection against somebody holding the SD card, and nothing on a Pi can be.

Untick it to get a session now and store nothing; you will need to come back and do this again when it lapses.

If this account has two-factor authentication, this cannot work — the appliance has no way to answer the prompt.
